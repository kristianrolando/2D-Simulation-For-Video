# Halaman Videos: teks, status, channel, video panjang

Pindahan dari README (isi asli, bahasa Inggris). Kapan dibaca: saat memakai halaman Videos, angka/filter per channel, atau membuat video panjang.

## Texts, status and tracking

Every render you start in the studio becomes an entry in **Videos** (the switch at the top of the left column, or press `V`). The entry is already filled in, so the form is never empty:

- **Texts for YouTube.** **Five titles** in different styles (direct question, what is at stake, starts with a number, curious statement, how/why explainer; click one to use it), a description, tags and exactly three hashtags (`#Shorts` first), written in English from the scene's own parameters (balls, rows, ratios, rule), its topic and the style: same video, same texts (seeded), and videos differ from one another. Titles are at most 100 characters with the hook and the main keyword first; the description opens with the hook and the keyword, explains what happens in two true sentences and has one calm subscribe line, in one of four layouts (**Another description** rewrites it). A **quality check** under the fields flags limits, duplicate tags, a missing keyword, bait phrases, claims the video does not show and numbers that are not in the video. Details: `docs/publishing.md`; the research behind it (patterns only, nothing copied): the research section of `docs/publishing.md`. Edit anything; counters and gentle warnings follow YouTube's documented limits (title 100 characters, description 5000 **bytes**, tags 500 characters in total with the commas, more than 60 hashtags are ignored, no `<` or `>`). The **Suggestions** menu in the Videos bar picks the language of the suggested title, description and tags: English (default) or Indonesian; it is stored in `data/videos.json` and does not translate the interface. **Copy** buttons sit next to each field and say "Copied ✓"; if the browser blocks the clipboard, the text appears selected in a box so Ctrl/Cmd+C works, and **Copy title, description and tags** at the top copies all three. **Reset to suggestion** puts the first suggestion back. Format: Shorts, 1080×1920, 60 fps.
- **Thumbnail and cover.** When a render finishes, the studio picks the best frame by itself (a busy, tense frame near the climax: the moment the pattern closes or the last ball lands) and draws two pictures in the video's own style with the hook on them: a **1080×1920 Shorts cover** (text in the safe zone) and a **1280×720 thumbnail** (text left, frame right), saved as JPEG in `data/thumbnails/`. The thumbnail is what the YouTube upload sets. In Videos you can edit **Text on the thumbnail**, press **Pick the best frame automatically** again, **Use this frame** for the frame the player is paused on, or **Without text**. A choice you made is never replaced by the automatic one. Free and local (ffmpeg and the canvas the renderer already uses).
- **Status and tracking.** Draft → Rendered → Scheduled → Published, a scheduled date, the published date (filled in with today when you mark it Published), the YouTube link and free notes. Changes save by themselves.
- **Shorts or Long.** The picker at the top (**All / Shorts / Long**, with counts) decides what the list, the status chips (their counts are per format; Shorts + Long = All), the search, the calendar and the CSV export show. The choice is remembered and is in the address: `#videos/long`, `#videos/shorts/calendar`, `#videos/long/build` (so a link works and the back button walks through your choices). The views are **List**, **Calendar** (the upload plan is for Shorts; long videos only show with a date you entered, marked "Long") and **Build long video** (see below). Long + List has a shortcut to the builder.
- **Toolbar.** Search (`/` focuses it, `Esc` clears it), simulation and style menus, sorting (newest, by schedule date, by title) and **More** (suggestion language, CSV of the chosen format, a JSON backup of everything, YouTube settings, the data folder). **Needs you** stays under the chips. "Clear filters" appears when nothing matches.
- **Renders not in the list.** A short banner says how many MP4s in `output/` are not in the list yet; **Add all N…** asks first and adds exactly the files that were counted; **Dismiss** hides it until that list of files changes.
- **List.** Filter by status, search titles, notes, scene, style and links. The top line says how many of the 60 scene × style combinations were never made into a video; open it for the map, and click a cell to open that scene and style in the studio.
- **Renders made outside the studio.** Opening Videos scans `output/` (the import call also accepts `{"files": ["name.mp4"]}` to add only some of them): every MP4 that has its `.job.json` next to it (`npm run render` writes one, with any `--out` name) and is not in the list yet is counted in a banner with one button, **Add all N to the list**. Nothing is imported by itself. Files changed in the last 15 seconds (still being written) are skipped, and a file already in the list is never added twice (matched by file name).
- **Two studios.** Only one studio may write to `data/`. It keeps `data/.lock` (its process id); a second studio on the same folder opens **read-only** with a red banner (you can look and copy, nothing is saved) and takes over by itself when the first one is gone. A lock left by a crashed or killed studio is recognised as stale and replaced.
- **Export.** CSV (opens in a spreadsheet), JSON (a complete backup copy) and **Open data folder**.

Fast path from render to published: **Render MP4** → **Texts & publish** (under the preview) → **Copy title, description and tags** → upload on YouTube yourself → **Published** → paste the link. That is 4 clicks and a paste.

Files are named `<scene>-<layout>-<style>-<timestamp>.mp4` in `output/`; **Play** and **Show in folder** are in the status strip under the preview (with **Texts & publish**), **Copy path** is in the render history (**All renders**), and the video's page has **Show MP4 in folder** with, right next to it, the picture buttons: **Show cover in folder** (Short: the 1080×1920 cover, the picture uploaded for Shorts), **Show thumbnail in folder** (Short: the 1280×720 picture, when it is a different file; long video: its 1280×720 thumbnail). A button with no picture behind it stays but is off, and its tooltip says why (no cover made yet, or the file is missing from `data/thumbnails/`). The status strip under the preview shows the MP4 only (a fresh render has no cover yet). The same buttons serve every screen from one function; the by-hand box below no longer repeats them.

**Where the data lives.** Everything is local, in `data/` (set `SIM_DATA_DIR` to move it), never inside `references/`:

```
data/videos.json        the list (carries "schemaVersion", plus the suggestion language)
data/.lock              process id of the studio that may write (removed on a clean stop)
data/backups/           a copy of the previous file before every save (newest 50) + one snapshot per day (60 days)
data/thumbnails/        thumbnails (<id>.jpg 1280×720) and Shorts covers (<id>-cover.jpg 1080×1920)
data/videos.damaged-*   a file that could not be read, kept untouched (also videos.newer-* for a newer schema)
```

Saving writes a temporary file, flushes it to disk and renames it over `videos.json`, so a crash or full disk leaves the old file whole. A missing or damaged file never stops the studio: the bad file is set aside, the newest good backup is restored (or the list starts empty), and a yellow notice in the Videos view says what happened. To go back by hand, copy a file from `data/backups/` over `data/videos.json`. The list itself never contacts YouTube and asks for no credentials; uploading is a separate feature (`docs/youtube.md`).

## Long videos (Videos → Build long video)

Joins finished Shorts into long 16:9 videos (1920×1080, 9–18 minutes, one theme each) with numbered clips, chapters, texts and a 1280×720
thumbnail, **ready to upload by hand** (the uploader only takes Shorts; long video upload is separate, later work). Pick **Build long video** in the Videos
page, **Suggest long videos**, create the drafts, **Build**. Builds run in the render queue, can be stopped and continued (finished clips are
reused) and survive a restart. Terminal: `node tools/compile.ts plan | build all | verify <slug>`. Details: `docs/compile.md`.

## Channels, numbers, filters and picking several

Every video carries a channel (its name; connected channels also carry the id). The page counts and filters by it.

- **One counting rule.** A number on the Shorts/Long picker, a status chip or an entry of the channel menu is **the number of rows you would see if you clicked it**, with every other filter that is on still applied (channel, format, status, search, simulation, style, "needs you"). So the channel entries (plus "No channel", when a video has none) always add up to "All channels", and a chip never promises rows the list will not show. The code is one pure place, `apps/studio/videos-view.ts`, tested in `tests/videos-view.test.ts`.
- **Channel menu:** `All channels · 509`, `Photon Lab · 264` … each with its number. **Channel strip** above the list: a line for the chosen channel (`Plasma Pendulum: 245 videos · 245 rendered · 0 scheduled · 0 published · 245 Shorts · 0 Long`) and one card per channel to compare them (total, status split, Shorts and Long); a click on a card filters to that channel, a click again goes back to all. The strip says what a channel holds in total (all formats and statuses); it is folded on a phone (the line stays) and the fold is remembered once you click it.
- **Filters are remembered** (channel, status, search, simulation, style; the format and the sort already were) in this browser (`localStorage`, wrapped in try/catch: without storage nothing is remembered and nothing breaks). A line under the toolbar says what is on (`229 of 494 shown · Channel: Photon Lab · Rendered`) with **Reset filters**; it shows only when something is on. A remembered channel that no longer exists falls back to All channels. An empty result says so, names the filters and offers the reset.
- **Search** finds title, description, tags, scene, style, status, **channel** and **id**; `/` still focuses it. **Sort:** last edited, render date, schedule date, title, duration, plus a button that turns the direction around.
- **Calendar** follows the chosen channel (only its videos and plan slots show; its line says so). The builder (*Build long video*) has no channel filter: it joins Shorts of any channel (see the finding in HANDOFF).
- **The banner "N renders in output/ are not in this list yet"** means: finished video files in `output/` that the studio never recorded as videos (made before tracking began, or outside the studio). *Add … to the list…* gives each a draft title and description (nothing is uploaded or deleted; the new entries get the active channel); *Hide* hides this exact list of files until it changes.
- **A channel added by hand:** More → *Add a channel by hand…* (or the link under the cards). It is a name only: no connection, no id, **the studio never uploads to it** (the upload check says so). Put videos on it with the video's *For channel* menu or *Channel for the videos shown…* (scheduled, published or uploaded videos keep their channel). The names live in the list file (`settings.manualChannels`, up to 12). A channel no video carries can be removed again from under the cards. This is how the videos of a channel run by somebody else (for example Fourier Loom) are grouped.
- **Pick several:** **Select…** puts a checkbox on every row; *Select all N shown* picks what the filters show. Then: **Mark N as uploaded by hand…** (one confirmation with the number and the channels; only *Rendered* videos without an upload record change; *Scheduled*, *Published*, uploaded and not-rendered ones are skipped and counted; nothing is sent to YouTube), **Undo the mark** (takes back only marks made this way), and **Export (CSV)** of the selection or of everything shown (`no, id, title, description, tags, channel, status, video_file, cover_file`; quotes, commas and line breaks inside a cell are quoted; UTF-8 with BOM). There is no bulk delete.
